How we work

  

    
01
Exposure Snapshot
A free 30-minute session. If CBAM does not apply to you, we say so and the conversation ends there.

    
02
Written scope
Every engagement begins with a written scope, a fixed price and a delivery date. We do not bill hourly.

    
03
Data collection
We supply the templates and work from your existing records. We do not ask you to build a data system before we can start.

    
04
Delivery and walkthrough
Deliverables are handed over with a working session, so your team can defend the numbers without us.

    
05
Standing behind it
We support the deliverable through your customer's or verifier's questions for 90 days at no extra charge.

  


  
  

    
What we will not do

    
We will not tell you that you face a fine from the European Union, because the CBAM obligation sits with the importer, not with you. We will not verify our own numbers, because the rules require that the party preparing the data and the party checking it are different. And we will not quote a saving before we have seen your data.
